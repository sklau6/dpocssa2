    Project nature

    Why this position exists

    T26

    This is a CSSA position responsible for supporting and operating multiple centralised Artificial Intelligence (AI) and data analytic platforms, including Chatbot-as-a-Service and the Social Media Text Analytics System.

    The position is required to evaluate the performance of the platforms to facilitate adoption by Government bureaux and departments (B/Ds), and to ensure smooth operations of the platforms. It sits at the intersection of technical delivery, service operations and cross-departmental coordination — translating a shared government capability into services that individual B/Ds can confidently adopt.

      • Document model changes, evaluation results and data-handling decisions.
